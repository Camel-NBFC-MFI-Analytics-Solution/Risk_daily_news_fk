# Risk_daily_news_fk
Real-time Streamlit dashboard for India Microfinance, JLG/SHG, NBFC and Climate Risk news. Pulls stories from Google News, Moneycontrol, ET, Business Standard, FE and RBI, removes bank news, and tags each story by risk category, severity and state. Also shows MFI/NBFC stock prices and lets you export CSVs.
